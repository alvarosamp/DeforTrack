The annotation study used the manual polygons for 248 matched source images
from three annotators. It did not use any of the inherited masks added to
validation or used as test replacements. The three pairwise scores were
averaged within each image before summarizing the 248 image-level averages.
Mean IoU was 75.52\%, and the approximate 95\% confidence interval was
73.43--77.61\% under an image-independence assumption. IoU against a
majority-vote consensus was 85.37\%, 85.97\%, and 89.17\% for annotators 1, 2,
and 3, respectively. The consensus includes each annotator's own contribution
and is not an independent reference.

% Add to the experimental protocol.
The expanded validation partition contains 892 image--mask pairs: the 833 retained
DeforTrack validation pairs and 59 pairs from Forest Aerial Images. The training
and internal test partitions were unchanged, yielding 12,861 training, 892
validation, and 892 test samples (14,645 after augmentation and 6,071 before
augmentation). The new masks were inherited from Forest Aerial Images and
converted to YOLO polygons. Rasterization of these polygons reproduced their
source masks with a mean IoU of 99.68\% and a minimum of 99.09\%. These values
measure conversion fidelity, not semantic correctness. Nine additions were
flagged for semantic review. The expanded split is an experimental derivative
and is distinct from the published Roboflow v3 split.

All 17 checkpoints were originally selected with the 833-image validation
partition. The expanded partition is used for evaluation of the frozen
checkpoints, not for checkpoint selection or retraining. The internal test
images, reference masks, and checkpoints are unchanged, so previously measured
internal results and computational profiles remain attributable to the original
evaluation protocol. The 59 added images were excluded from the Forest Aerial
external benchmark, leaving 4,963 of the previous 5,022 pairs. The external
metrics were re-aggregated from the existing per-image scores after confirming
the retained image and mask hashes. Amazon/Atlantic Forest retained all 599
pairs. These screens do not establish geographic or temporal independence.

With weights fixed, all 17 models scored lower in mean IoU on the expanded
validation partition than on its original 833-image subset. The decreases
ranged from 3.04 to 3.47 percentage points. YOLOv11x remained the highest
scoring checkpoint, with 89.86\% on the original subset, 41.51\% on the 59
additions alone, and 86.66\% on the expanded validation partition. This
comparison reflects the change in evaluation samples, not a change in
checkpoint weights. Recalibrating the weighted fusion rule on the expanded
validation partition again selected the threshold of 0.05.
